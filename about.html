Hi, I’m Pravesh. I am a researcher working in computer architecture, with a particular focus on virtual memory and address translation. Much of my academic curiosity revolves around how modern systems move, translate, and manage data efficiently beneath the surface of computation.

This page is a place to document my research journey and the ideas, questions, and experiences that shape how I think. Outside research, I enjoy cycling, running, trekking, chess, stories, philosophy, mindfulness, consciousness, and meditation.

I hope to explore both the technical and the personal: the architecture of machines and the architecture of the mind.
