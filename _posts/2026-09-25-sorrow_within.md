<strong>The Sorrow Within</strong>

<p>
If I tell you of the sorrows in my heart,
I fear they will burn my tongue.</p>

<p>
If I keep them inside,
I fear they will burn me from within.
</p>

<p>
And if I let them out,
I fear
they will burn the whole world.
</p>
